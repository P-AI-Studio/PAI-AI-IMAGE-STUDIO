# Community Code of Conduct

The project aims to provide a respectful place to report defects, discuss model behavior, and propose changes.

Expected behavior:
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique code and evidence, not people.
- Share only logs and images you have permission to disclose.
- Do not post passwords, access tokens, private model credentials, or other people's personal data.
- Follow the repository's license and third-party model/runtime terms.

Harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory abuse, and deliberate exposure of private data are not acceptable. The project maintainer may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors or users.

To report a conduct concern, contact the repository maintainer through a private channel rather than creating a public issue containing sensitive details.
